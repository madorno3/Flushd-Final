Quintal Script is a cool, bold brush typeface.

Drawn and created by Mans Greback & Sarwo Edi during the years 2019-2021, this flowing lettering has a wavy style and a vivid personality. 

Perfect for a product or company logo, a poster or any artistry; this typographic masterpiece is sure to bring character to your brand. 

Use underscore _ for a beautiful swash. 
Example: Quintal_

Use multiple underscores for different swashes. 
Example: Hand____
(Download required)

Quintal Script includes three different styles. In addition to the Regular weight, it also comes as Thin as well as Bold, giving the type an extra dimension of versatility. 

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and has a guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more features; all to give you full control and customizability. 
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europe to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. 
It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
